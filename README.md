Measures graph-Laplacian smooth and residual components and section disagreement energy for any bounded risk field.


## Open mathematics, paid computation (original Shop x402 family)

The existing numeric scalar projection remains in src/measure.mjs. Two **new**
public exports in src/assay.mjs use SignGraph's original cellular-sheaf
mathematics, not an independent replacement solver.

- **Finite sheaf cohomology**: exact rational H0/H1, coboundary rank,
  and optional source-record-indexed section extension or linear obstruction.
- **Finite sheaf Laplacian**: the same exact cohomology plus separately
  qualified IEEE-754 sparse Laplacian and optional Dirichlet energy.

Input admission is bounded to 16 vertices, 32 edges, 48 coordinates,
dimension 4 per stalk, finite numerical states and strictly rational
restriction entries. These operations only cover a finite 1-dimensional
cellular chart over rational vector spaces. They do not claim H2, arbitrary
Grothendieck sheaf descent, certified spectra or physical/financial prediction.

The existing x402 Autosell producer reads the **source-owned operation
schemas** from the exported assay catalog and non-code commerce terms from
package.json's x-market.offers. These are two candidate operations:

A manifest is not an installed payable API, a live 402, or a sale. Both

No new payment wallet, database, catalog or storefront is created.

A signed triangle with three negative restrictions is a useful independent
negative control: the exact H0 and H1 both vanish, even though the corresponding
numerical Laplacian has positive eigenvalues 1, 1, 4. Exact cohomological rank,
numerical spectra, source-indexed extension and formal kernel proofs are
different evidence types. Source-generated Lean hypotheses and comparator
theorems must be independently qualified before claiming strict improvement
over Hansen–Ghrist's spectral theory.

### Revenue-release admission

1. Native install and all original SignGraph + assay tests must pass, with an
   independent exact linear algebra oracle and dimensional/metric/overflow tests.
2. Autosell must derive the offers against the original engineering seller
   record, not handwritten NI identities. Held endpoints must issue **no 402**.
3. Measure worst-case CPU, RAM, egress, facilitator costs and positive margin
   before approving actual prices.
4. Qualify a Shop-owned real 402, customer-authorized payment, useful result and
   attributable receipt. Bazaar/Market must preserve the **same original**
   seller and settlement identity without double-charging.

The mathematics and source are open. The paid product is reproducible,
bounded execution, with exact results and limitations honestly attributed.
